WHO WE ARE

Who we are

Established in April 2015, Work Station is based in Hanoi, Vietnam. Through discovering and developing technical talent, Japanese-language and business etiquette education, and practical design and development support, we help Japanese companies with recruitment and technical operations.

Working with the Japanese company 株式会社Seta Engineering, we connect client communication, proposals, contracts and project management in Japan with our talent, education and technical delivery base in Vietnam. By combining the roles of both companies, we support the entire process from defining hiring requirements and finding and recommending talent to pre-employment education and follow-up after joining.

OUR STRENGTHS

The strengths of Work Station

01COMMUNICATION

Quality and communication for Japanese companies

We emphasize not only technical quality but also communication with Japanese companies, including progress updates, reporting, information sharing and consultation. Our coordination framework, including Japanese-language support, reduces misunderstandings about specifications and requirements.

02TALENT & DELIVERY

Connecting talent development with technical delivery

Alongside Japanese-language and business etiquette education and training tailored to company requirements, we provide technical outsourcing such as lab-based development, BIM and construction DX. Combining talent development and practical support, we help build the capabilities Japanese companies need.

03JAPAN PARTNERSHIP

Collaboration with Seta Engineering in Japan

Established in February 2026, 株式会社Seta Engineering acts as the client contact in Japan, handling proposals, contracts, requirement definition, project management and operational support. Working with Work Station’s talent, education and technical base, it connects recruitment with post-employment retention support.
