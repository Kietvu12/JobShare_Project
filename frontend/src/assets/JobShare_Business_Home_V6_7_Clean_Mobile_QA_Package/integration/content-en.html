FAQ

Questions before you begin

Q.Is Workstation’s recruitment agency service required?+
A.

No. With Direct Scout, companies can search for candidates and contact them directly. Choose the hiring method that fits your team.

Q.Can we delegate candidate outreach and interviews?+
A.

Yes. With Delegated Scout, Workstation can handle outreach, interviews, interest checks and recommendations.

Q.Can we receive referrals from recruiting partners?+
A.

Yes. Jobs can be opened to recruiting partners so candidates can be referred through the partner network.

Q.Can candidates from multiple hiring channels be managed together?+
A.

The existing JobShare Business design supports reviewing hiring source and selection status by job and candidate.
