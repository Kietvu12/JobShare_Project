# JobShare Business Home V2 — Prompt ảnh đề xuất

## 1) Hero main visual (cần làm lại)
**Mục đích:** thay ảnh hero hiện tại bằng ảnh đúng tinh thần JobShare Business, gần nhịp ReadyCrew hơn.

**Prompt:**
Photorealistic Japanese B2B website hero image of a professional East Asian businesswoman in a navy or dark charcoal suit, warm confident smile, subtle pointing gesture toward the headline area, standing pose, clean studio cutout, premium corporate look, friendly but trustworthy, suitable for a recruitment platform homepage hero, no text, no logo, transparent background.

## 2) Final CTA visual (cần làm lại)
**Mục đích:** ảnh nhân vật cho CTA cuối trang, cảm giác khuyến khích liên hệ / đăng ký.

**Prompt:**
Photorealistic East Asian businesswoman in a light gray or beige suit, smiling warmly, slight thumbs-up or welcoming gesture, elegant standing pose for a Japanese corporate CTA section, clean cutout, transparent background, premium B2B marketing style, no text, no logo.

## 3) About / technical talent image (nên làm lại nếu muốn đồng bộ hơn)
**Mục đích:** minh họa nguồn nhân lực kỹ thuật và tuyển dụng xuyên biên giới.

**Prompt:**
Photorealistic corporate website image showing a small team of East Asian and Vietnamese technical professionals collaborating in a bright modern office, discussing engineering recruitment or project matching, natural expressions, premium Japanese B2B visual style, clean composition, no logos, no text baked into image, horizontal layout.

## 4) Recruitment consultation image (nên làm lại nếu muốn đồng bộ hơn)
**Mục đích:** minh họa phần Workstation support / tư vấn tuyển dụng.

**Prompt:**
Photorealistic Japanese corporate recruitment consultation scene, HR consultant and company representative discussing hiring needs in a clean modern meeting room, professional but approachable atmosphere, premium B2B website photography, natural expressions, no logos, no text, horizontal composition.

## 5) Mid-page CTA banner illustration (có thể thêm sau)
**Mục đích:** nếu muốn CTA giữa trang sinh động hơn.

**Prompt:**
Flat 2D Japanese business illustration banner showing several professionals discussing hiring and matching opportunities, simple clean line art with blue and yellow accents, off-white background, premium corporate web style, friendly and trustworthy, no text, wide horizontal composition.
