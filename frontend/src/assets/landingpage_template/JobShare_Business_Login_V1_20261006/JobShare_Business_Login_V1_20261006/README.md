Business Login package forked from the latest CTV/Candidate login structure.

- Same two-column layout/component family as the latest JobShare login pages
- Business 6.7 blue theme
- Text brand `JOBSHARE BUSINESS` (no logo asset)
- Business visual asset `assets/login-business.webp`
- VI / EN / JA entry files
